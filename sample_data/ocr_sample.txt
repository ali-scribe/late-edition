THE EVENING HERALD
NEW YORK, MONDAY, JANUARY 1, 1900.

THE CENTURY OPENS WITH BRIGHT PROSPECTS.
Thc twentieth century wns ushered in at midnight amid scenes of enthusiastic celebration throughout the entire city. Crowds gathered at Broadway and Forty-second Street to welcomc the new era with shouts, bells and horns. The poliee arrangements were admirable, and no serious disorders were reported by Chief Devery.

At the City Hall Mayor Van Wyck receivcd members of the Municipal Council and distinguished visitors from neighboring states. In a brief address, the Mayor congratulated the citizens upon the marvelous growth of Greater New York during the past year and predicted continued prosperity for all industries.

REPORTS FROM EUROPEAN CAPITALS.
Dispatches receivcd by cable early this morning indicate that the advent of the new year wns observed quietly in London and Paris. The commercial outlook is regarded as favorable, though anxiety still prevails concerning operations in South Africa. Lord Roberts and General Kitchener arrived at Gibraltar yesterday en route to the Cape.

A special dispatch from Berlin states that Emperor William addresscd the officers of the garrison, expressing his hope for international peace and the advancement of German commerce during the coming decade.
